## 1. Mine facts

### 1a. Zhomart (Kazakhmys), Ulytau region, Karaganda oblast - underground copper-sulphide, room-and-pillar with pillar recovery
- Opened 2006; "объём добычи составляет 3,6 млн тонн в год" (3.6 Mt/yr copper-sulphide ore); "более тысячи человек" (>1,000 workers); fly-in/fly-out "по 15 дней в две смены" (15-day rotation, two shifts). [F] https://bes.media/amp/rudnik-zhomart-vozobnovil-rabotu-posle-gibeli-semerih-gornyakov-v-ulitauskoy-oblasti-f60cb4/
- Incident depth: 640 m. [F] same bes.media URL; also [F] https://s.thepeninsula.qa/article/17/02/2025/kazakhstan-mine-collapse-traps-seven ("around 640 metres (2,100 feet)")
- Conflict: a search summary cited "700 meters" for Zhomart depth [S] https://timesca.com/seven-killed-in-kazakhstans-latest-mining-tragedy/ . Two fetched sources say 640 m. Treat 640 m as the incident depth; the mine's maximum working depth is [U].
- Mining method: room-and-pillar with pillar recovery (repeated geotechnology). [S] NU thesis: https://nur.nu.edu.kz/items/bf71e4ad-8030-4cfd-bd74-ef65e9161c46 (Gabdullin, 2024, Bachelor, NU School of Mining; compares room-and-pillar with pillar recovery vs Cut & Fill vs Sublevel Stoping; concludes room-and-pillar ranks lower because of geotechnical problems; full PDF not readable here, abstract has no numbers) [F]
- Pillar and geomechanics (KSTU annotation, Zhomart): claims panel-pillar system since 2006, repeated pillar recovery since 2009, rising man-made seismic activity in re-mining zones from August 2017, causes including local pillar recovery with small spans and "опорное давление от зависших пород на неотработанные целики". [S] via search summary of https://www.kstu.kz/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ANNOTATSIYA-rus-1.pdf . The PDF itself was not extractable (binary, 7 pages, Word 2016 creation metadata) [F]. Unverified.
- Access (shaft/decline), ramp gradient, horizon elevations, LHD fleet, drift sizes: NOT FOUND in any source read.
- Ventilation and atmosphere (2026): 200 m3/s capacity increase after main-fan repairs; a ventilation borehole to 600 m depth; three Korfmann units and six VME fans bought; investment 306.9 million tenge; a third ventilation shaft under construction (no date). [F] https://bizmedia.kz/2026-09-18-kazahmys-usilil-kontrol-rudnichnoj-atmosfery-i-ventilyacziyu-na-zhomarte/
- Gas monitoring (2026): stationary gas analysers 24/7 to a central dispatch; 130 portable analysers; 62 self-propelled underground machines with automated methane monitoring; equipment is locked out automatically when a methane threshold is exceeded. [F] same bizmedia URL.
- Personnel positioning and emergency alert: 58 km of radiating cable (излучающий кабель) supports the personnel positioning and emergency alert system. [F] same bizmedia URL.
- Geomechanical monitoring: a unified digital system deployed July 2026 ("В июле 2026 года на руднике внедрена единая цифровая система геомеханического мониторинга"); first stage laser scanning with mobile RS10; "миллионы пространственных точек". Built with TOO "Soyuz Geo Mekh". [F] https://informburo.kz/novosti/cifrovoi-monitoring-dlia-bezopasnoi-ekspluatacii-rudnika-zomart-vnedriaet-kazaxmys (10 Jul 2026). Also [F] bizmedia 2026-09-18 above.
- Safety programme: 45 measures, 40 done; 2.7 billion tenge. [F] bizmedia 2026-09-18.

### 1b. ERG Donskoy GOK - "10th Anniversary of Kazakhstan Independence" mine (chromite; DNK)
- Commissioned 2001. Stage 2 deepening planned to 1000-1200 m. [F] https://rudmet.ru/journal/1177/article/20055 (Til V.V., Bekeev M.M., Sashurin A.D., Gornyi Zhurnal 2013 No. 5: "The «10-th anniversary of Kazakhstan independence» mine was set in exploitation in 2001." and "The second stage of this mine foresees the deepening of underground mining to 1000-1200 m.")

Unit counts not given. [F] https://im-mining.com/?p=105409 (report dated 2 Oct 2024). The Epiroc press release URL https://www.epirocgroup.com/en/media/corporate-press-releases/2024/20241008-epiroc-wins-large-underground-mining-equipment-order-in-kazakhstan returned 403 [U on date: URL says 2024-10-08].

## 3. Kazakhstan safety rules for the dispatcher decision

### 3a. Current Rules: Order No. 352 of 30.12.2014
- Title: Правила обеспечения промышленной безопасности для опасных производственных объектов, ведущих горные и геологоразведочные работы; Minister for Investment and Development order No. 352, 30.12.2014. Source: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1400010247 (adilet returned no body text; the body was read from the mirror https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V1400010247 , fetched in 5 chunks by offset). Search summaries mention later amendments: Order 772 of 07.11.2018 (adds para 4-4) and MChS order 382 of 14.07.2023 (amends para 1). Current redaction not verified [U].
- Paragraph numbers below are from the mirror text (WebFetch summaries). Check against adilet before quoting in a submission.

Evacuation / withdrawal:
- п. 8 (Раздел 1): supervisor who cannot remove a hazard stops work and "выводит работающих в безопасное место". [F-summary]
- п. 4 (Раздел 1, ПЛА): the ПЛА must cover "пути вывода людей, застигнутых авариями в шахте, из зоны опасного воздействия". [F-summary]
- п. 605 (Ch. on rockbursts/ground): on signs of self-collapse, "людей выводят в безопасное место". [F-summary]
- п. 1575 (fire): if ventilation stops, people "немедленно выводятся на поверхность". [F-summary]
- п. 851 (harmful gas 0.5% or more): work stops, people moved to fresh air, power cut, mine dispatcher notified; re-entry needs technical head's permission. [F-summary]
- п. 883 (negligible-gas mines, fan stop): stop over 30 min, people evacuated from workings served by that fan to fresh-air workings; stop over 2 h, personnel moved to surface. [F-summary]
- п. 884: fan reversal completed within 10 min, airflow in main workings at least 60% of normal. [F-summary]
- п. 890: sudden fan stop: dispatcher, technical head and others notified. [F-summary]
- п. 758 (main fan stop): people brought to fresh air. [F-summary]
- п. 761 (gas found): the person taking the reading withdraws people from workings. [F-summary]

Dispatcher and alarm:
- п. 54: mine dispatcher sends individual messages to every underground worker (code, text or voice); "Время оповещения не более 4-5 минут"; system continuous before, during and after an accident; locating people under rubble ("обнаружение человека ... под завалом"). [F-summary]
- п. 58: a supervisor who cannot fix a hazard reports to the mine dispatcher. [F-summary]
- п. 1603 (fire): reports immediately to technical head or mine head and to the mine dispatcher. [F-summary]

Self-rescuers:
- п. 52: isolating self-rescuers issued before descent; mine stock exceeds the largest shift by 10%; quarterly serviceability checks; half-yearly training. [F-summary]
- п. 337(2) (Ch. 6): isolating self-rescuers in number exceeding max underground shift by 10%. [F-summary]
- п. 1623: everyone in hazardous zones (water/gas breakthrough) carries an isolating self-rescuer. [F-summary]
- Protective time (minutes) for the stock: NOT found in the Rules text read.

Refuge chambers:
- п. 122 (Ch. 2, Subsection 2 §3): refuge chambers "При обосновании проектом" (only where the project justifies them); "камеры аварийного воздухоснабжения" store spare self-rescuers. [F-summary]
- п. 904(3): refuge and emergency air chambers marked on ventilation plans; no operational rule. [F-summary]

Exits:
- п. 115: at least two separate exits per active mine. п. 116: spacing at least 30 m (20 m for non-combustible). п. 122: autotransport ramps may serve as emergency exits under stated conditions. п. 124: emergency-exit workings inspected at least monthly. п. 130: each working block has at least two independent unobstructed exits. [F-summary]

Rockburst and ground control:
- Rockburst chapter, пп. 664-752 (Subdivision 3): categories "Опасно"/"Неопасно" (п. 671); п. 679 "Горные удары относятся к авариям и подлежат расследованию"; п. 682: a ПОР for liquidation of consequences of a rockburst; п. 697: seismic stations in complex conditions; пп. 704-712 forecast and prevention. [F-summary of zakon.uchet.kz mirror, chunk 200000-300000 region]
- п. 640(3): geomechanical monitoring of the worked-out space during pillar recovery; п. 594-595: pillar/roof stability calculation and geotechnical service recommendations. [F-summary]
- п. 44: rock-burst propensity assessment required. [F-summary]
- п. 131-132: support per approved passports; work stops until passport revised; revision within 24 hours of a change. [F-summary]

Plan of liquidation of accidents (ПЛА):
- п. 3: ПЛА prepared by the technical head, reviewed yearly with rescue services; requirements in Appendix 1 (not in the text read). [F-summary]
- п. 4: ПЛА has an operational part and division of duties; rescue measures; approved with the rescue service head. [F-summary]
- п. 47: all mines served by the mine rescue service (ПАСС ОПБ) during construction, operation and liquidation. [F-summary]
- п. 844: mines in one ventilation system share one ПЛА. п. 885: fan reversal test act attached to the ПЛА. [F-summary]
- п. 1866 (dredges): ПЛА approved 15 days before season. Not relevant to mines.
- The operational-part text of the ПЛА (Appendix 1 and the section named "Основные требования по оформлению оперативной части плана ликвидации аварий") was NOT found in the mirror text (covers paragraphs 1-2103; text ends mid-paragraph 2103 near the appendices). [F] Not verified.

Speed assumptions and time to safety: NOT found in any Rules text read.

## 4. Real incidents (Kazakhstan underground ore mines, ground instability and evacuation)

- 17 Feb 2025, Zhomart (Kazakhmys), 640 m, seven miners killed. Sequence: initially a rock-mass collapse reported with cable breaks and no contact [F] https://s.thepeninsula.qa/article/17/02/2025/kazakhstan-mine-collapse-traps-seven and https://www.currenttime.tv/a/33318662.html (Russian-language fetched summary); Kazakhmys then said a natural gas explosion was the preliminary cause, "uncharacteristic" of its mines [F] https://www.kt.kz/eng/incidents/preliminary_cause_for_kazakhmys_mine_collapse_announced_1377974576.html . Government commission (chaired by Kanat Bozumbayev) preliminary cause: methane accumulated in a working, ignited by an electrical spark [S] https://informburo.kz/novosti/osnovnuiu-pricinu-tragedii-na-saxte-kazaxmysa-v-ulytauskoi-oblasti-nazvali-v-pravitelstve . Bes.media (fetched) states: methane-air mixture ignited by non-explosion-proof electrical equipment; "установила стопроцентную вину работодателя" (commission found the employer 100% at fault) [F] https://bes.media/amp/rudnik-zhomart-vozobnovil-rabotu-posle-gibeli-semerih-gornyakov-v-ulitauskoy-oblasti-f60cb4/ . Resumption permitted 16 June 2025 [F] same bes.media. Conclusion for the twin: the official cause was gas, not ground failure. Use Zhomart as a ground-instability analogue only with that caveat.
- 24 Aug 2018, Tishinsky mine (Kazzinc, Ridder), 15 m working, ground subsided and collapsed; LHD machinist buried with his machine; 40 people and 5 machines in rescue; ore mining suspended; search later, machinist not found [F] https://informburo.kz/novosti/deyatelnost-rudnika-v-vko-gde-pod-zavalami-ishchut-mashinista-priostanovlena.html (26 Aug 2018) and [F] https://newtimes.kz/proishestviya/77379-dve-nedeli-pod-zavalami-v-vko-kaztsink-vyrazil-soboleznovaniya-rodnym-gornyaka (6 Sep 2018; "under rubble for two weeks"). Very close to the dispatcher scenario (LHD operator in a collapse zone, rescue vs withdrawal). Cause not established in sources read.
- 5 Jan 2020, Ridder-Sokolny (Kazzinc), 16th horizon of "Вторая юго-западная": work section blocked by rock mass; a drill operator trapped; contact via borehole on 6 Jan; company said all underground staff brought to surface under emergency procedure and flooded section isolated [F] https://www.inbusiness.kz/ru/news/burilshik-ridder-sokolnogo-rudnika-v-vko-bolee-sutok-ostaetsya-pod-zemlej (6 Jan 2020). The same article cites: Nov 2013 rock collapse at Tishinsky/Ridder 13th horizon killed 2 of 5 miners [F, within the same page as a cited history line]; April 2014 sinkhole; Aug 2018 loader driver missing 12-day search.
- Ridder-Sokolny 2020 and Tishinsky 2013/2014: the exact mine for the 2013 collapse is stated as "the mine's 13th horizon" in the 2020 article; check before citing.
- 2024 Donskoy GOK (Khromtau): worker died after falling in Nov 2024 [S] https://bizmedia.kz/2024-11-11-v-hromtau-rabochij-pogib-posle-padeniya-na-donskom-goke/ (not a ground-stability event).
- Abaiskaya (Qarmet, coal, Karaganda): 155 miners evacuated after power failure; date not in summary [S] https://bes.media/news/v-karagandinskoy-oblasti-evakuirovali-155-shahtyorov-iz-shahti-abayskaya-947902/ . Coal, not ore: low relevance but a real evacuation example.
- Lenin mine (ArcelorMittal, coal), 3 Nov 2022: five killed, 106 evacuated [S] https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/11/3/methane-blast-kills-at-least-five-workers-at-kazakhstan-coal-mine . Coal, methane.
- No 2024-2025 ore-mine rockfall with a documented evacuation found beyond Zhomart. A Zhomart "shaft collapse, miners survived" report from November 2025 [S] is from an activist/news source only.

## 6. Recommendation

### 6a. Best named analogue
- Primary: Zhomart (Kazakhmys, Ulytau). Why: underground, two shifts, 640 m incident depth, room-and-pillar with pillar recovery (the geomechanical situation a dispatcher must manage), published 2025 fatal event and 2026 monitoring programme (personnel positioning cable, methane lock-out, ventilation), 3.6 Mt/yr, >1,000 workers. Gaps: horizon elevations, two-horizon/ramp layout, LHD fleet, workers per shift, gradient all not found.
- Caveat: the official cause (methane explosion, electrical spark, employer 100% at fault per the commission) means it is not a pure ground-failure case. Model it as a "gas + ground" dispatcher case, or use it only for the monitoring and withdrawal context.
- Alternative for the LHD and ground-collapse scenario: Tishinsky (Kazzinc), 2018 subsidence with an LHD operator buried. No depth, levels or fleet numbers were found. Good for the event narrative.
- Alternative for fleet and ramp layout: ERG DNK (10th Anniversary). Battery Epiroc ST14 SG and MT42 SG fleet, sub-level caving, 1000-1200 m deepening plan. Numbers: 7.5 Mt/yr design (im-mining). No unit counts, no levels.
- Molodezhnaya: depth 450-550 m (1981), useful as older-depth reference only.

### 6b. Numbers we can take directly, with sources
- Zhomart: 3.6 Mt/yr; >1,000 workers; 15-day rotation, two shifts; opened 2006; incident depth 640 m; incident 17 Feb 2025, 7 killed; resumed 16 June 2025. Source: bes.media (above).
- Zhomart 2026: 58 km radiating cable; 130 portable and fixed gas analysers; 62 machines with methane lock-out; 600 m ventilation borehole; 200 m3/s capacity increase. Source: bizmedia 18 Sep 2026.
